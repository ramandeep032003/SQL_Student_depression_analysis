Key Insights
Total Students - 4,464

Average Age - 22.6

Average Semester of Studies - 5.4

Percentage depressed students - 26.9%

1. Depression rates are fairly similar across genders. Female students have a depression rate of 26.60%, while male students have a slightly higher rate of 27.27%. The difference is small, so gender does not appear to be a major distinguishing factor in this dataset.

2. Depression rates vary across home divisions. Rangpur has the highest rate at 31.96%, followed by Rajshahi at 31.56% and Khulna at 31.02%. Dhaka has the lowest rate at 22.40%. This shows that the proportion of students reporting depression differs by division.

3. Younger students have a slightly higher depression rate. The rate is 27.68% among students aged 17–23, compared with 24.98% among those aged 24–28. The difference is noticeable but not large.

4. Most depressed students in the dataset have a CGPA of 2.75 or above. Around 91% of depressed students fall into this group, compared with 94% of students classified as non-depressed. This suggests that a lower CGPA is not the only factor associated with depression.

5. The number of depressed students is highest in the middle-class family-background group. There are 731 students in this group, compared with 352 from lower-middle-class and 118 from rich-class backgrounds. These are counts, not depression rates, so they should not be interpreted as showing that middle-class students are more likely to experience depression.

6. Average spending without study differs by family background. Average spending in depressed students are higher among students from rich-class backgrounds than among those from middle-class and lower-middle-class backgrounds. 

7. Friend-circle status is common among depressed students. Of the 1,201 students classified as depressed, 773 have friend_circle = true, while 428 have friend_circle = false. This is a count-based comparison and does not establish whether having a friend circle is linked to a higher or lower depression rate.

8. Average CGPA is quite similar across health-condition categories. The averages range from approximately 3.39 to 3.43, suggesting little difference in average CGPA between these groups in this dataset.
